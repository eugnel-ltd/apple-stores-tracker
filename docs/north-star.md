# North star

Draft from README, 27 Oct 2026; the owner confirms

Help Apple buyers compare one exact configuration across 15 official Apple Store markets in pounds, with tourist tax-refund estimates and price history. Show UK refurbished alternatives and disclose configuration differences.

The web channel is live at `apple-price-tracker.eugnel.com`. The site is free, independent and not affiliated with Apple.

Priorities: compare like configurations, show source and quote times, and state uncertainty in refund and refurbished estimates.

## Non-goals

- No user accounts or sign-in.
- No payments or checkout.
- No guarantee of tax-refund eligibility or savings.
